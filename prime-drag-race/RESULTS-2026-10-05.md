## What this does and does not show

- It shows a correct, reproducible measurement of one small workload on a Linux-hosted GB10, with receipts anyone can re-hash.
- A prime sieve result is not overall AIEN performance. It says nothing about AIENOS, models, or other workloads.
- On this workload the native GB10 path is about 20x slower than CUDA running the same algorithm (median ratio 20.3x), and it is also slower than the single-thread CPU baseline (median ratio 2.13x). The sieve is a small, memory-bound job, so launch overhead and the native path's maturity matter more than raw chip speed.
- Run-to-run spread is small (CV under 1% for all three), so the gaps are not noise.
